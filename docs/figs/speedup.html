Speedup vs compute threads

Dashed line = ideal linear speedup. The heat3d checkpoint-generation line improves up to 8 threads, then saturates. Matmul softens later as allreduce and fault-pool contention grow with p.

Amdahl / Karp-Flatt serial-fraction model

Empirical serial fraction e = (1/S - 1/p)/(1 - 1/p) per point. Flat or small e means clean scaling; rising e means the serial part grows with p.
